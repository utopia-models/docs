# ADR-10765 — The goal is income by any path that covers Cairo living, remote first

## Context and Problem Statement
ADR-10103 (2026-10-24) set the bet as "a paid engineering job in a city Tyler chooses", with NYC, DC, San Diego,
remote and Egypt (any job) all running ungated. On 2026-10-30 Tyler narrowed and reframed it in one day:
"No more on-site jobs. Remote only." then "Honestly positions in NYC and DC metro let's keep. Cause i would take those
right now." · "let's get me paid now" · and in the `/north-star` interview: "Income by any path", "Any 1099 work is
going to Utopia Models, Inc. so it's all the same thing", Cairo: "focus on remote US work or just remote engineering
work in general", success = "Cover Cairo living", Kratos: "Keep it while it lasts".

## Decision Outcome
**Income by any path, enough to cover Cairo living (~$1–2K/month) without touching savings.** Jobs, contracts and
Upwork count equally; contract (1099) income flows through Utopia Models, Inc., so building the company is the same
goal. **Targets: remote engineering work (US or anywhere) first; on-site or hybrid only in the NYC and DC metros.**
San Diego, other US metros and Egypt on-site/any-job are dropped. The Kratos salary continues while it lasts, so new
income is additive. Interviews already in progress (Apex, Capital One, E-Space) continue as practice and possible
offers. The fleet itself is in scope: upgrade and optimize it, focused on NET.

### Consequences
- `net/work/pipeline/criteria.json` and `net/README.md` § The criteria change to remote + NYC + DC (#10765).
- Upwork (ronin), contracts and remote applications are one income funnel, not separate bets.
- Supersedes ADR-10103's geography (San Diego, Egypt any-job) and its job-only goal.
