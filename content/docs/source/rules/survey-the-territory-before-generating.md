# When the output must land with an outside audience, census that audience FIRST. The census is the deliverable, not the scaffolding.

## Fires when
Producing anything judged by people outside the fleet (name, resume bullet, landing page, cold email, pitch, product description) when material about US is to hand. 🔴 **The tell is that the generating step feels ready to start** — I am working supply-side. Also: *"propose N options"*, a brief whose first verb is invent/draft/write/generate, **editing an artifact produced the wrong way** (a revision inherits its shape).
🔴 **A site build** (2026-10-17): open every reference site Tyler names FIRST and match its structure. Media and copy are RIPPED from successful competitor sites — never stock, never authored; take the strongest ranked headline; every claim keeps its source. ## The rule

1. **Survey before generating, and make the survey the WHOLE job.** A census run as a secondary task
   becomes scaffolding for the invention; the report's weight lands on the invented thing. Dispatch the
   survey alone, read it, and only then generate.
2. **The generator must be BLIND to our material.** A subagent told what we have will find a way to use
   it. If it asks what we have, the method is working.
3. **The artifact is a set of TEMPLATES with placeholders, committed, before anything is filled.** Not a
   mental step — a file. And the check is per-slot: *"this fills template X, which answers this quoted
   audience language."* If both cannot be named, the work is an edit.
4. **Where nothing in our material fits a slot, the slot stays empty.** Never substitute the closest
   thing we have; that is the supply side re-entering through the back door.
5. 🔴 **Familiar beats distinctive when the goal is a DECISION by a person.** Match the register the
   audience already reads. Distinctiveness belongs in the content, never in the shape.

## Cost when it fired

**PLAN-179 (2026-08-16):** returned 12-18 invented names with rationales; the top three were rejected
outright. Tyler's correction named the method, not the output: *"run research on other AI Ads Agencies
to figure out what names they have."* The case was written the same day and **sat unabsorbed for 11
days** — there was no room in the corpus, which was at 19,997 of a 20,000 B ceiling that had never been
measured.

**PLAN-225 (2026-08-25 → 27):** the same failure, five repetitions, three days. *"You are way too
focused on me, instead of the person reading this resume… BULLET POINTS SHOULD BE 90% DONE BEFORE EVEN
LOOKING AT MY KRATOS WORK OR FLEET INFRASTRUCTURE!!!!"* Then, after a measured fix that swapped one
noun: *"Feel like you are STILL NOT reverse engineering."* Tyler's standard: *"You should be able to run
research and write bullet points and content in ONE PASS, not require 3 days of iterations"* — and
*"these are not things that I should need to catch."*

**When it was finally done correctly**, the census produced findings unreachable from the supply side:
only 2 of 12 target postings mention out-of-work evidence at all · they count *calls, developers, users,
documents/day* and **zero of twelve count commits or lines of code** · 58.5% of hired-candidate bullets
claim no business outcome, so demanding one from every bullet reads as unlike the resumes that worked.
