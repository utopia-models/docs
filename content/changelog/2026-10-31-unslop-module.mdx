[Module: cut AI tells from your writing with unslop](/docs/modules/unslop) is live.

- Prompt 1 measures which coding agents you run (Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Cursor,
  Aider), where each reads skills, and where your writing lives. It asks at most three questions.
- Prompt 2 installs the pinned skill file and its MIT license, adds an always-on last-pass line if you
  want one, rewrites one of your drafts and checks that every number and link survived.
- A "No coding agent?" section covers ChatGPT and Claude in the browser.
